Interests

I've always been interested in a wide range of topics, which is one of the reasons I went into investing. I started my career covering utilities and alternative energy before moving into a generalist role, where I've looked at everything from biotech and semiconductors to bubble tea chains.

Around 2024, I started becoming very interested in the human brain and increasingly fascinated by the possibilities of neuroengineering. What interest me the most are non-invasive brain-computer interfaces and my long-term goal is to help develop devices that can treat diseases and improve people’s quality of life.

To work toward that goal, I am currently pursuing a part-time master’s degree in Neuroengineering at Johns Hopkins University while working as an investment analyst at a hedge fund. I have also spent a lot of my time reading, learning, and building new projects, and it's been a lot of fun!

Hobbies

Basketball is my favorite sport, and I try to play at least once a week. The 2013–14 San Antonio Spurs are my all-time favorite team (Go Spurs Go!). I also love watching baseball, although I'm pretty terrible at playing it myself...

Outside of sports, I watch a lot of movies, TV, and YouTube. Some of my favorites are Fight Club, Breaking Bad, and Outdoor Boys.

I also love traveling and try to visit new places whenever I get a longer break. South America and Africa are the only continents I haven't been to yet (other than Antarctica), and hopefully I'll get to explore both soon!
